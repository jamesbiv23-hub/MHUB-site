FAQ

Questions

Is MHUB free?

MHUB itself is free. It runs inside Claude, so you sign in with a Claude account, and the AI tutor uses your own Claude plan's usage.

Who can see my data?

Only you. Each account's mistakes, scores, flashcards, notes and settings are stored privately for that account.

Can I bring my Anki deck?

Yes. Export it from Anki as "Notes in Plain Text (.txt)" and import it in the Flashcards tab. Reviews use spaced repetition inside MHUB.

Does it include AAMC or UWorld questions?

No. MHUB works alongside the practice you already use. The tutor writes original MCAT-style questions, and your log stores your own notes on what you missed.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. MHUB works in any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer.
